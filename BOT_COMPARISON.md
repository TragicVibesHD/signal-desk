# Signal Desk compared with established trading systems

Reviewed October 3, 2026. This is a comparison of engineering practices and evidence, not a ranking of promised returns. We have not verified a publicly available bot with independently audited, comparable day-trading profits for this $2,000 cash account.

## Changes implemented

- New dated sessions freeze broker protection with engine version 4. Existing plans keep their previous rules; they are never silently migrated. Entry limits remain cash capped. Bracket prices are rounded from the entry limit, not from an assumed eventual fill price. Rounded stop distance must fit the entry risk budget.
- Protective leg identities enter the durable ledger through the app's own parent order. External orders are never adopted by stock symbol alone. Child fills reduce owned shares and update allocated cash/P&L; duplicate nested results do not double-count a fill.
- Time, strategy, loss and manual exits persist an exit request, cancel app orders, reconcile, and sell only remaining owned shares. Pending cancellation or an uncertain response defers the sale. An existing market exit is not cancelled and resent each poll. Missing/inactive protection halts new entries and schedules closure. Partial entries with held legs take this same fail-closed path.
- A double protective fill produces an ownership conflict and an attention state, never a false completed-session result. The app does not attempt to trade its way out of an unexpected short position.
- Research aggregates partial exits into completed position cycles. Win rate, profit factor and average trade P&L use these completed cycles. Net P&L still includes residual marked holdings, which are reported separately. Removing the single largest winner tests concentration; it is a screening heuristic, not a significance test.
- Research includes keep-cash and equal-weight whole-share stock-basket comparators. The basket holds overnight and uses different risk/exposure; it is disclosed and excluded from strategy selection.
- Reports count expected five-minute calendar slots, gaps, off-grid timestamps and completely absent sessions. Missing bars are never fabricated. A 95% coverage screen is an explicit chosen heuristic; IEX gaps can reflect no trades on that exchange.
- Research liquidity uses the preceding completed bar, not the execution bar's unknown final volume. For OHLC brackets, a gap below the stop fills at the worse opening price; when both thresholds are touched after an open inside the bracket, the stop takes precedence. Only one partial exit consumes a bar's configured exit participation. Bid/ask timing, fill queues and the broker's exact election sequence remain unmodeled. Interactive legacy replay retains its original close-threshold model; the research report records its different execution assumptions.
- Operational checks show connection, reconciliation, accounting and protection status. Actual paper session comparisons retain completed results. Quote measurements are unavailable for older orders; none are invented.

## What would count as progress

First connect paper credentials locally and run the real-data comparison. Keep every strategy row, including losses and stressed costs. Freeze the chosen profile before prospective paper observation. Record multiple sessions, costs, drawdowns, rejected orders, fills and outages. A candidate must survive cost stress and concentration checks, not merely have a green backtest.

The recorded September 30 result remains $1.20 on $2,000 before fees, with five entries. There is no real-data performance improvement established by this implementation session because credentials were unavailable. Fixture and synthetic results only validate software. The AI decision layer is still absent: adding model calls would add another hypothesis and cost, not independently establish an edge.

Signal Desk is now a stronger paper research application. It is not an audited production trading platform, and its profit objective remains an experiment rather than a promised outcome.
